# SAP Employee FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

## IT Support & Technical Issues

### Q: How do I submit an IT support ticket?
**A:** You can submit IT support tickets through the SAP IT Service Portal:
- **Portal URL:** https://helpdesk.sap.com/service-portal
- **Emergency Support:** Call +1-800-SAP-HELP (727-4357)
- **Login:** Use your SAP employee credentials
- **Categories:** Hardware issues, software installation, network problems, account access

### Q: How do I request new hardware or software?
**A:** Submit equipment requests through the IT Service Catalog:
- **Equipment Request Form:** https://helpdesk.sap.com/equipment-request
- **Software License Request:** https://helpdesk.sap.com/software-licenses
- **Approval:** Requests require manager approval for items over $500
- **Delivery Time:** 3-5 business days for standard equipment

### Q: I forgot my SAP password. How do I reset it?
**A:** Use the Self-Service Password Reset portal:
- **Reset Portal:** https://identity.sap.com/password-reset
- **Alternative:** Contact IT Help Desk at +1-800-SAP-HELP
- **Requirements:** You'll need your employee ID and registered phone/email
- **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Required after password reset

### Q: How do I connect to the SAP VPN?
**A:** Follow these steps to access SAP VPN:
- **VPN Client:** Download from https://vpn.sap.com/client-download
- **Setup Guide:** https://internal.sap.com/vpn-setup-guide
- **Credentials:** Use your SAP domain credentials
- **Support:** Contact IT if you encounter connection issues

## Onboarding & New Employee

### Q: How do I access my onboarding tasks?
**A:** Your onboarding dashboard is available through SAP SuccessFactors:
- **Login URL:** https://successfactors.sap.com
- **Dashboard:** Navigate to "Onboarding" from the main menu
- **Tasks:** Complete items under "For You Today" section
- **Help:** Contact your HR representative or manager for assistance

### Q: Where can I find my employee handbook?
**A:** The employee handbook is available in multiple locations:
- **SharePoint:** https://internal.sap.com/employee-handbook
- **SuccessFactors:** Learning → Company Resources
- **HR Portal:** https://hr.sap.com/handbook
- **Mobile App:** SAP Employee app under "Resources"

### Q: How do I enroll in benefits?
**A:** Benefits enrollment is done through the HR portal:
- **Benefits Portal:** https://benefits.sap.com
- **Enrollment Period:** Within 30 days of start date
- **Help:** HR Benefits team at benefits@sap.com
- **Phone Support:** +1-800-SAP-BENEFITS

### Q: What is my employee ID and where can I find it?
**A:** Your employee ID is available in several places:
- **SuccessFactors Profile:** Personal Information section
- **Payslip:** Top right corner of pay statements
- **Badge:** Printed on your employee access badge
- **HR Contact:** Ask your HR representative if you can't locate it

## Time & Attendance

### Q: How do I submit my timesheet?
**A:** Time entry is done through SAP SuccessFactors:
- **Time Tracking:** Employee Central → Time → Time Sheet
- **Deadline:** Submit by Friday of each week
- **Approval:** Manager approval required
- **Support:** Contact HR for time entry issues

### Q: How do I request time off?
**A:** Submit leave requests through SuccessFactors:
- **Request Portal:** Employee Central → Time Off
- **Advance Notice:** Submit requests at least 2 weeks in advance
- **Approval:** Requires manager approval
- **Policy:** Review time-off policy in employee handbook

### Q: What are SAP's holidays?
**A:** SAP holiday schedule includes:
- **Holiday Calendar:** https://hr.sap.com/holidays
- **Regional Variations:** Varies by country/location
- **Floating Holidays:** 2 personal days per year
- **Updates:** Calendar updated annually in December

- **SAP Analytics Cloud:** https://analytics.sap.com

### Q: How do I access my pay information?

## Workplace Services

### Q: How do I book a meeting room?
**A:** Meeting room booking through Outlook:
- **Outlook Calendar:** Add room as attendee
- **Room Finder:** Use "Room Finder" in Outlook
- **Capacity:** Rooms available from 2-50 people
- **AV Equipment:** Contact facilities for special equipment

### Q: Where is the cafeteria and what are the hours?
**A:** Cafeteria information varies by location:
- **Main Campus:** Building A, Level 1
- **Hours:** 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Monday-Friday)
- **Menu:** https://dining.sap.com
- **Payment:** Badge swipe or mobile app

### Q: How do I report a facilities issue?
**A:** Facilities issues can be reported through:
- **Facilities Portal:** https://facilities.sap.com/service-request
- **Phone:** +1-800-SAP-FACILITIES
- **Emergency:** Call building security immediately
- **Types:** HVAC, lighting, cleaning, maintenance

## Security & Compliance

### Q: How do I report a security incident?
**A:** Security incidents should be reported immediately:
- **Security Portal:** https://security.sap.com/incident-report
- **Phone:** +1-800-SAP-SECURITY
- **Email:** security@sap.com
- **Types:** Data breach, suspicious activity, lost badge

### Q: What is SAP's clean desk policy?
**A:** SAP's clean desk policy requires:
- **Documents:** Lock away confidential documents
- **Screens:** Lock computer when away from desk
- **Visitors:** Don't leave visitors unattended
- **Compliance:** Regular security audits conducted

### Q: How do I get a visitor badge for guests?
**A:** Visitor registration process:
- **Visitor Portal:** https://visitors.sap.com/register
- **Advance Notice:** Register visitors 24 hours in advance
- **Requirements:** Government-issued ID required
- **Escort:** Visitors must be escorted at all times

## Career Development

### Q: How do I find internal job postings?
**A:** Internal positions are posted on:
- **Career Portal:** https://careers.sap.com/internal
- **SuccessFactors:** Career Development → Job Search
- **Manager Discussion:** Talk to your manager about opportunities
- **Networking:** Attend internal career fairs

### Q: How do I request a performance review?
**A:** Performance reviews are conducted through:

- **Schedule:** Annual reviews in Q4, mid-year check-ins in Q2

- **Documentation:** Prepare self-assessment beforehand

### Q: What is SAP's mentoring program?
**A:** SAP offers formal mentoring programs:
- **Mentoring Portal:** https://mentoring.sap.com
- **Application:** Open twice yearly (January and July)
- **Duration:** 6-12 month programs
- **Types:** Career mentoring, technical mentoring, leadership development
