SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding FAQ & Quick Reference
==================================================

This document provides concise, practical answers and links for common questions about SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding, IT support, and essential employee processes. Use this as a quick reference for new hires, managers, and HR partners.

---

**About SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding**
--------------------------------------
SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding helps internal and external new hires quickly become productive members of the organization. It provides tools for:
- Completing onboarding tasks and forms
- Accessing and signing documents (tax forms, contracts, NDAs, etc.)
- Tracking onboarding progress via the Onboarding Dashboard
- Engaging with managers and HR throughout the process

**Accessing the Onboarding Dashboard**
--------------------------------------
- Go to: https://successfactors.sap.com
- Log in with your SAP credentials
- Select "Onboarding" from the main navigation menu
- View your assigned tasks and progress under "For You Today"

**Initiating Onboarding**
------------------------
- Manual: Use Action Search → "Add New Hire to Onboarding"
- Recruiting: From candidate profile, select "Initiate Onboarding"
- Internal Hires: Triggered by job change in Employee Central or Recruiting

**Typical Onboarding Steps**
---------------------------
1. Complete personal and employment information
2. Review and sign documents (tax forms, contracts, NDAs)
3. Enroll in benefits (https://benefits.sap.com)
4. Complete compliance and background checks
5. Attend orientation and training sessions
6. Meet your manager and team
7. Access required systems and tools

**Document Management**
----------------------
- All onboarding documents are available in the Onboarding Dashboard
- Sign documents using SAP SuccessFactors e-Signature or DocuSign
- Download completed documents for your records

**Manager & HR Tasks**
---------------------
- Review new hire data and documents
- Assign onboarding tasks to new hires
- Track onboarding status for all team members
- Add new hires to Employee Central after onboarding is complete

**IT & System Access**
---------------------
- Request IT equipment: https://helpdesk.sap.com/equipment-request
- Submit IT support tickets: https://helpdesk.sap.com/service-portal
- Reset password: https://identity.sap.com/password-reset
- VPN setup: https://internal.sap.com/vpn-setup-guide
- Access SAP systems: Use links in your onboarding checklist or ask your manager

**Learning & Training**
----------------------
- Access SAP Learning Hub: https://learning.sap.com
- Complete required onboarding courses
- Track learning progress in SuccessFactors → Learning → My Learning

**Benefits & Payroll**
---------------------
- Enroll in benefits: https://benefits.sap.com
- View pay statements: SuccessFactors → Employee Central → Pay
- Update direct deposit: HR portal or Employee Central

**Time & Attendance**
---------------------
- Submit timesheets: SuccessFactors → Time → Time Sheet
- Request time off: SuccessFactors → Time Off
- View holiday calendar: https://hr.sap.com/holidays

**Facilities & Workplace**
-------------------------
- Book meeting rooms: Outlook Calendar → Add Room
- Report facilities issues: https://facilities.sap.com/service-request
- Cafeteria info: https://dining.sap.com

**Security & Compliance**
------------------------
- Report security incidents: https://security.sap.com/incident-report
- Clean desk policy: Lock documents/screens when away
- Register visitors: https://visitors.sap.com/register
